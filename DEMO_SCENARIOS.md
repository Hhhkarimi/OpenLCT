# OpenLCT Demo Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Peak Sales Day

Input:
- Increased inbound volume
- Warehouse queue growth
- Higher store demand

System Response:
- Detect congestion risk
- Prioritize incidents
- Recommend slot changes

## Scenario 2: Cold Chain Risk

Input:
- Cold vehicle shortage
- SLA pressure

System Response:
- Identify affected stores
- Recommend fleet reassignment

## Scenario 3: Last Mile Surge

Input:
- Increased online orders
- Courier availability pressure

System Response:
- Predict ETA risk
- Recommend courier allocation
